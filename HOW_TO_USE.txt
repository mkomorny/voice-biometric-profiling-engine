HOW TO USE: voice-biometric-profiler
====================================

PURPOSE
  Generate ultra-deep, exhaustive, forensic-grade voice biometric and acoustic profile reports.
  Serves as the master physical and acoustic source/filter spine for voice cloning, parametric
  synthesis, speaker verification, and clinical monitoring.

CAPABILITIES
  - 10-section exhaustive acoustic and biomechanical analysis
  - Fundamental frequency (F0) distribution, range, stability, and glottal flow dynamics
  - Time-domain waveform properties (symmetry, zero-crossing, envelope attack/decay)
  - Frequency-domain spectral characteristics (harmonic structure, tilt, HNR, formants F1-F4)
  - Perturbation metrics (jitter, shimmer, CPPS, GRBAS perceptual scores)
  - Supraglottic resonance profiles and anatomical correlates (VTL, laryngeal tension)
  - Master parameter table for voice synthesis (Kokoro, Klatt, WORLD, VITS)

USE CASES
  - "Give me the forensic acoustic breakdown of this person's voice"
  - You need the master biometric dossier before building satellite skills
  - Calibrating parametric or neural TTS models to clone an individual's vocal timbre
  - Forensic speaker comparison and acoustic voice authentication

IDEAS
  - Run recording-chain-measurement-ingest first to provide calibrated empirical numbers
  - Pair with speaking-personality-profile for complete 360-degree acoustic + behavioral coverage
  - Feed section 10 parameters directly into synthesis-control-compiler

PROMPTS YOU CAN PASTE
  /voice-biometric-profiler Queens-male-orator

  create voice profile for Steve Jobs

  detailed voice biometric report on 30yo adult male with Down syndrome

  analyze this voice like the DS profile. Give me the complete 10-section acoustic breakdown.

  give me the forensic acoustic breakdown of this speaker from the provided audio.
