# Voice Biometric Profiler

## Purpose

This skill generates ultra-deep, exhaustive, forensic-grade voice biometric and acoustic profile reports. It enforces uncompromising scientific rigor, multi-paragraph descriptive depth, exact numerical specificity, physiological and biomechanical mechanisms, time-domain waveform properties, frequency-domain spectral characteristics, articulatory phonetics, dialectal realization modeling, and full replicability standards (engineered directly for voice cloning, parametric/neural TTS synthesis, forensic speaker comparison, and acoustic research).

Reports produced by this skill must be expansive, comprehensive, and exhaustive—never abbreviated or summarized.

---

## Activation Triggers

Activate automatically when the user:
- Asks for a voice profile, acoustic profile, or biometric report (e.g., *"create voice profile for [subject]"*, *"detailed voice biometric report on [person]"*).
- Invokes `/voice-biometric-profiler [subject]`.
- Asks to analyze or profile a voice based on an audio description, video clip, character concept, celebrity, or demographic breakdown.
- Uses phrases like *"analyze this voice"*, *"voice biometric profiler on [subject]"*, *"give me the forensic acoustic breakdown of [speaker]"*.

## Mandatory Report Schema & Structure

Every generated profile MUST follow this comprehensive 10-section format with full preamble and extended notes. Each sub-heading within sections must be thoroughly developed with multi-sentence technical analysis and concrete numerical metrics.

```markdown
VOICE BIOMETRIC & ACOUSTIC PROFILE
Profile Type / Condition: [Baseline / Normal State, Performance/Rally Context, Controlled Studio, or Clinical Environment; specify adaptation parameters for emotional loading vs. formal registers]
Subject / Voice Description:
[Comprehensive introductory background paragraph: demographic details (age, sex, build, stature), vocal pathology status, native language and regional/metro dialect, decades of vocal usage and loading history, stylistic presentation and persona, respiratory support, biomechanical factors, and idiolectal consistency across registers]

1. Fundamental Frequency (F0) & Glottal Source Characteristics
- **Mean F0 and Range:** [Median/mean F0 in Hz and semitone reference relative to C0/A440; context-dependent shifts; conversational vs. performance excursion range in Hz and semitones; demographic normative comparison]
- **F0 Stability & Variation:** [Pitch standard deviation in Hz/semitones, coefficient of variation; jitter trajectory over sustained speech; macroprosodic vs. microprosodic dynamic contouring; pitch floor, ceiling, and falsetto/break excursions]
- **Glottal Source & Phonation Type:** [Primary phonation type (modal, pressed, creaky, breathy); glottal closed quotient (Qc / CQ %) and open quotient (Qo / OQ %); attack characteristics; cycle duration T0 in ms; glottal flow pulse skewness and derivative dU/dt]
- **Waveform Periodicity:** [Cycle-to-cycle regularity, subharmonic bifurcation, additive perturbation during pressed/gravel moments]
- **Physiological / Biomechanical Mechanism:** [Intrinsic/extrinsic laryngeal muscular tension (TA/CT balance); vocal fold cover-body compliance; subglottal pressure Psub in cm H2O; laryngeal height and posture during phonation]

2. Time-Domain Waveform Properties
- **Overall Waveform Shape & Glottal Pulse:** [Quasi-periodicity; glottal pulse symmetry/asymmetry quotient; steepness of closing phase; open phase duration; return phase ripple and supraglottic turbulence]
- **Amplitude Envelope & Dynamics:** [Overall RMS energy, peak-to-peak amplitude modulation; dynamic range in dB (whisper to maximum projection); attack and decay dynamics (rapid vs. sustained)]
- **Micro-variations:** [Cycle-to-cycle amplitude perturbation (shimmer); textural roughness and acoustic grit]
- **Noise Component:** [Additive turbulent aspiration noise; transglottal leakage distribution across the cycle; glottal chink vs. supraglottic sources]
- **Zero-Crossing Behavior & Envelope Decay:** [Voiced vs. unvoiced zero-crossing rates (crossings/s); envelope damping and transient release characteristics]

3. Frequency-Domain & Spectral Characteristics
- **Harmonic Structure:** [Harmonic series definition up to 3–5 kHz; energy balance; H1 vs H2 difference (H1-H2 in dB) as indicator of glottal adduction]
- **Spectral Tilt / Slope:** [High-frequency roll-off in dB/octave; Hammarberg Index (dB); Alpha Ratio (0–1 kHz vs 1–5 kHz in dB); spectral brightness vs. warmth]
- **Harmonics-to-Noise Ratio (HNR) and Related Metrics:** [Global HNR in dB; high-frequency HNR (>2 kHz); variations with sustained phonation and vocal loading]
- **Formant Frequencies & Vowel Space:** [Exact F1–F4 means, ranges, and bandwidths (B1–B4) in Hz for neutral vowels; accent-driven vowel space shifts; Euclidean Vowel Space Area (VSA) compression/expansion; lip-rounding and pharyngeal tuning effects]
- **Resonance Profile:** [Oral-pharyngeal coupling; nasal resonance/hyponasality/hypernasality; extra nasal formants (FN) or antiformants/zeros; head vs. chest vs. pharyngeal focus]
- **Long-Term Average Spectrum (LTAS):** [Global spectral envelope; energy peaks supporting projection (speaker's formant cluster at 2.8–3.5 kHz); noise floor distribution]
- **Additional Spectral Features:** [Source-filter interaction; spectral moments (center of gravity, skewness, kurtosis) for fricatives and release bursts]

4. Perturbation & Voice Quality Metrics
- **Jitter:** [Local jitter (%), RAP (%), PPQ5 (%); stability over sustained vocalization]
- **Shimmer:** [Local shimmer (%), local shimmer (dB), APQ3/APQ5/APQ11 (%)]
- **Cepstral Peak Prominence (CPP / CPPS):** [CPP in dB for sustained vowels and connected speech; harmonic organization measure]
- **Other Perturbation or Spectral Measures:** [Noise-to-Harmonics Ratio (NHR), Voice Turbulence Index (VTI), Soft Phonation Index (SPI)]
- **Perceptual Voice Quality (GRBAS & Expanded Descriptors):** [Grade, Roughness, Breathiness, Asthenia, Strain scores (0–100 or 0–3); expanded qualitative descriptors: gravelly, raspy, pressed, resonant, authoritative, etc.]
- **Integrated Voice Quality Impression:** [Synthesized forensic narrative on the combined perceptual-acoustic impression of the voice]

5. Prosodic & Temporal Modulation of the Waveform
- **Speaking Rate & Rhythm:** [Overall words per minute (wpm), articulation rate in syllables/second (sps); isochrony, stress-timing vs. syllable-timing]
- **Pitch Variation & Intonation:** [Nuclear pitch accents, level nuclear tones, high-rising terminals, declarative drop slope in semitones; falsetto spikes and rhetorical pivots]
- **Loudness & Dynamics:** [Mean conversational SPL (dBA at 1 m); dynamic range (ΔSPL in dB); strategic volume modulation for emphasis, intimacy, or confrontation]
- **Pause, Phrasing & Timing:** [Mean pause duration in ms; pause-to-speech ratio; strategic multi-second rhetorical pauses; vowel prolongation ratios on stressed targets]
- **Stress, Accent & Rhythmic Feel:** [Syncopation, emphatic stress density, repetitive cadence, oratorical bounce]

6. Linguistic, Dialectal & Phonetic Characteristics
- **Consonant Features:** [Allophonic realizations; stop consonant Voice Onset Time (VOT in ms) and closure durations; fricative spectral peaks; liquid/glide rhoticity or non-rhoticity metrics; dentalization, glottalization, th-stopping]
- **Vowel Features:** [Specific vowel shifts and mergers (e.g., THOUGHT raising/gliding, TRAP-BATH split, short-a tensing, low-back mergers); diphthong trajectory flattening or extension; duration lengthening]
- **Prosodic & Intonational Features Specific to Variety or Style:** [Dialect-specific tune contours, boundary tone configurations, cadence patterns]
- **Code-Switching / Feature Suppression:** [Contextual variability; shift between formal reading and extemporaneous/informal performance]
- **Articulatory Precision & Transitions:** [Articulatory force, locus equations, formant transition speeds (ms), intelligibility under rapid delivery]

7. Anatomical, Physiological & Lifestyle Correlates
- **Vocal Tract Anatomy Influences:** [Vocal tract length (cm), pharyngeal and oral cavity proportions, palate arch, laryngeal framework dimensions]
- **Muscle Tone & Function:** [Laryngeal hyperfunction/hypotonia, supraglottic squeezing, ventricular fold recruitment, extrinsic laryngeal muscle involvement]
- **Phonation Mechanism Notes:** [Glottal efficiency, aerodynamic resistance, mucosal wave propagation dynamics]
- **Respiratory Support & Subglottal Pressure:** [Vital capacity, thoracic/diaphragmatic breathing support, Psub maintenance, endurance across long utterances]
- **Lifestyle / Habitual & Performative Factors:** [Decades of vocal loading, public speaking demands, hydration, environmental irritants, learned rhetorical behaviors]
- **Health / Condition Impacts (Baseline Context):** [Age-related structural changes (presbyphonia, ossification), reflux/allergies, structural nasal resistance, baseline stability]

8. Variability, Contextual Factors & Consistency
- **Intra-Speaker Variability:** [Consistency of core biometric markers across sessions; fatigue effects (pitch ceiling drops, breathiness/roughness rise)]
- **Inter-Context Differences:** [High-energy rally/performance vs. controlled studio/interview vs. conversational baseline]
- **Environmental & Recording Influences:** [Microphone proximity, room reverberation, background acoustic noise, Lombard effect]
- **Perceptual Consistency Across Listeners:** [Reliability of listener identification, psychoacoustic cues of authority, emotional resonance, and authenticity]

9. Overall Sound-Wave & Perceptual Signature (Replicable Description)
[Dense, multi-paragraph technical and perceptual synthesis.
Paragraph 1: Acoustic waveform, glottal source characteristics, harmonic ladder, spectral tilt, formant architecture, and perturbation texture.
Paragraph 2: Perceptual impression, psychoacoustic presence, tone, authenticity, and communicative authority.]

10. Key Replicable Parameters for Voice Analysis, Synthesis, or Comparison
- [Bullet points summarizing exact quantitative parameters: F0 mean/range, jitter/shimmer/HNR, formant targets, resonance filters, prosodic metrics, phonetic shifts]
- **Recommendations for Voice Conversion / Cloning / TTS:** [Concrete guidance on source-filter modeling, glottal pulse shaping, spectral tilt compensation, nasal filtering, and prosodic rules]
- **For Forensic Comparison:** [Primary invariant biometric identifiers, acoustic stability metrics, and distinctive idiolectal markers]
- **For Clinical / Functional Monitoring:** [Key indicators for vocal fatigue, tissue changes, respiratory efficiency, and loading management]

Optional Extended Notes:
- **Emotional or Loaded State Variations:** [Shifts during high arousal, anger, fatigue, intimacy]
- **Comparative Benchmarks:** [Acoustic contrasts with demographic or peer reference profiles]
- **Biometric Uniqueness:** [The specific multi-parameter intersection that makes this voice unique]
- **Synthesis / Modeling Recommendations:** [Architecture-specific advice: VITS, HiFi-GAN, FastSpeech2, diffusion-based TTS, or voice conversion]
- **Clinical or Functional Implications:** [Endurance, loading risks, vocal hygiene accommodations]
```

## Generation Rules

1. **Maximum Exhaustive Depth**:
   - Write out complete, deeply detailed paragraphs for all sections.
   - Do NOT truncate, abbreviate, or use one-line summaries.
   - Every metric must have quantitative values (Hz, dB, %, ms, sps, wpm, semitones).

2. **Physiological & Biomechanical Grounding**:
   - Explicitly link acoustic properties to anatomical structures (vocal fold mass/tension, subglottal pressure, pharyngeal volume, velopharyngeal port dynamics, mandibular excursion).

3. **Demographic & Contextual Accuracy**:
   - Accurately scale metrics to the subject's age, biological sex, health conditions, dialect, and communication context.

4. **Clean Output**:
   - Output ONLY the formatted profile. Do not include introductory pleasantries or meta-chatter.
